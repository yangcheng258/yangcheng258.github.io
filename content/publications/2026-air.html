Builds a community-assets index via spatial Bayesian factor analysis from 84 candidate variables narrowed to 39, across 3,100 contiguous U.S. counties. Documents that 1,946 rural counties (46.1%) sit far from where their assets would predict — Spearman ρ = 0.29 between QoL and asset ranks — motivating a four-type rural typology in which different community types "breathe different air."
