Asks how the spatial determinants of wellbeing vary across U.S. counties. Builds spatially-informed wellbeing composites using GWPCA (6-variable and 4-variable Isserman subsets, adaptive bandwidth bw = 2,647), with planned sPCA and GWR extensions plus LISA cluster maps. Six deprivation indicators — poverty rate, high rent burden, no high-school diploma, unemployment, unhealthy physical days, PM2.5 days — drawn from ACS, County Health Rankings, EPA, BLS, and the USDA rural typology, across ~3,100 counties. NARSC conference version already presented; in revision for the RSS rural-livability special issue.
