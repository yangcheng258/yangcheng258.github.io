Research theme · 03

Migration & place choice

How do life-cycle stage, housing market conditions, and place characteristics shape where people move and why? This newer thread takes the spatial-livability framing into household decisions over the life course — asking which places attract college-educated movers, which retain low-income residents, and how housing constraints reshape those flows for older adults.

Question

What moves whom, when, and where to?

Existing migration research often treats movers as undifferentiated, or splits them only by skill. This thread starts from life-course stage — early-career, family formation, retirement — and asks how each stage interacts with housing-market conditions and place amenities to produce the migration patterns we observe.

The literature review brings together life-course migration theory, place-livability work (which is where this thread connects to Theme 01), housing-constraint research, and recent work on demographic subgroups including college-educated movers, low-income households, and older adults.
