How to reach out

Mention which of these your note is about — it helps me sort and reply faster.

For collaboration

Research collaboration

Working together on rural livability, the green economy, or migration. Most useful to send up front:

  • The research question you're chasing
  • Methods or data you have access to
  • Where in the timeline you are
  • Constraints (deadlines, funding, IRB)
For office hours / catch-up

Coffee or a catch-up

Just to talk — about research, methods, or navigating academic life. Email a few days ahead and:

  • Suggest two or three windows that work for you
  • Note in-person (Madison) or video
  • A line on what you'd like to discuss
For invitations & press

Invited talks, briefings, press

For invited talks, panels, briefing requests, or podcast/press inquiries, please mention up front:

  • The audience and format
  • Expected length and the rough date
  • Outlet or affiliation, and any deadline
