Research theme · 01

Rural livability & quality of life

What makes a rural county a good place to live, and how can we measure it without falling into the laundry-list trap? This thread reframes quality-of-life work from the demand side (what people choose) to the supply side (what places possess), grouped under "community capitals." It compares seven index-construction techniques head to head, asks how housing burden and food insecurity compound to shape rural health outcomes, and stays insistently spatial in its methods.

Section A · Measurement & methods

Measuring quality of life & livability

How to build defensible composite measures of quality of life and rural livability — the methodological backbone underneath everything else in this thread.

A
2026Article

Multidimensional measures of quality of life: a county-level methods comparison

Cheng, Y., Conroy, T. & Deller, S.
Papers in Regional Science 105, 100129.Published
PDF DOI Code Cite
›

Side-by-side audit of seven index-construction techniques (simple average, PCA, GWPCA, sPCA, factor analysis, Bayesian FA, spatial Bayesian FA) for U.S. county-level QoL measurement. The first head-to-head comparison of these methods on a single 3,100-county dataset, with eleven sensitivity analyses (S01–S11).

Section B · Livability & wellbeing

Livability & wellbeing

Substantive analyses applying the methods above to map rural America's livability landscape and the spatial determinants of community wellbeing.

B
2026Working paper

Rural Livability: What's in the Air?

Cheng, Y., Conroy, T. & Deller, S.
Working paper, targeting Regional Studies.In progress
PDF DOI Code Cite
›

Builds a community-assets index via spatial Bayesian factor analysis from 84 candidate variables narrowed to 39, across 3,100 contiguous U.S. counties. Documents that 1,946 rural counties (46.1%) sit far from where their assets would predict — Spearman ρ = 0.29 between QoL and asset ranks — motivating a four-type rural typology in which different community types "breathe different air."

rural
2026Working paper

Spatial Determinants of Wellbeing: a county-level analysis

Cheng, Y.
Revising for Regional Studies, Regional Science rural-livability special issue.In revision
PDF DOI Code Cite
›

Asks how the spatial determinants of wellbeing vary across U.S. counties. Builds spatially-informed wellbeing composites using GWPCA (6-variable and 4-variable Isserman subsets, adaptive bandwidth bw = 2,647), with planned sPCA and GWR extensions plus LISA cluster maps. Six deprivation indicators — poverty rate, high rent burden, no high-school diploma, unemployment, unhealthy physical days, PM2.5 days — drawn from ACS, County Health Rankings, EPA, BLS, and the USDA rural typology, across ~3,100 counties. NARSC conference version already presented; in revision for the RSS rural-livability special issue.
